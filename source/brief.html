Desk Buddy

A small animated character that floats on your Windows desktop, on top of every app, and reminds you to drink water, focus and take breaks.

Windows 10 / 11 Works offline On / Off anytime 5 clips ready
1 · Character

The character

The original drawing, and the same drawing placed on a green background. The green version was the starting image for every clip in Google Flow, so the background can be removed and she floats on the desktop.

5 · Requirements

Features

  • Water reminderTimer counts from when she's turned on. At the interval she drinks, shows the bubble and plays a soft chime.
  • Water counter"Done, I drank" adds one glass. Shows 3 / 8 style progress. Resets every morning.
  • Focus timerStart focus begins a countdown. She stays quiet until it ends. Stop cancels it.
  • Break timerStarts after focus with the stretch clip. Skip goes back to idle.
  • On / OffPower button on her control bar, or the tray icon near the clock. Off pauses all timers and hides her.
  • Floats on topAlways visible over Word, Chrome, VS Code and other apps. No window border or background.
  • Drag to moveDrag her anywhere on screen. She remembers the spot next time.
  • MuteTurns off the chime. Reminders still show.
  • SettingsChange water interval, daily goal, focus and break length, size.
  • Start with WindowsoptionalOpens by herself when the laptop starts. Off by default.

7 · Quality

Battery, privacy and limits

Must have

  • Light on battery: when Off, no animation and timers paused.
  • Animation pauses when the screen is locked.
  • No internet needed. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Water count and settings saved only on your laptop.
  • Works on Windows 10 and 11.

Known limits

  • Windows shows a "protected your PC" warning the first time, because the app isn't signed. Click More info, then Run anyway.
  • Download is about 100 MB because it includes its own mini browser.
  • Reminders only work while the app is running.
8 · Build

Tech and install

Tech

  • Python + ffmpeg: remove the green background, export transparent video.
  • HTML, CSS, JavaScript: bubble, buttons, timers, sound.
  • Electron: turns it into a Windows app with a transparent, always-on-top window and a tray icon.

Install

  1. Download DeskBuddy.zip and unzip it.
  2. Double-click DeskBuddy.exe.
  3. On the blue warning, click More info, then Run anyway (first time only).
  4. She appears in the bottom-right corner.
